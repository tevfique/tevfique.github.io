Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

sepet is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data it accesses

With the owner's permission, sepet uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel, set their publish time, and read public search results and public comments related to a video topic.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to make and publish the owner's videos. It is processed on GitHub Actions in the owner's private repository. Run outputs are kept for at most 7 days, and copies used to resume a failed run expire automatically. Research results are kept in the owner's private repository until the next research run replaces them, and never longer than 30 days: older ones are deleted automatically.

Public statistics of public videos (such as view counts and publish dates) and figures the owner derives from them may be kept for up to 36 months; derived figures are calculated by sepet, not provided by YouTube. Other YouTube data, such as video titles and comment text, is deleted or refreshed within 30 days.

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. sepet does not use cookies and does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.

Google and YouTube

sepet uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
