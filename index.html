sepet

sepet is a personal automation tool. It prepares explainer videos for its owner's own YouTube channel and uploads them on a schedule.

What it does with your Google account

  • Uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and schedules their publish time.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail of those videos.
  • Reads public YouTube search results and public comments about a video topic, to plan better videos.

It is used only by its owner. It does not sign in other users and does not access anyone else's account.
